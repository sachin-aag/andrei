NUMBERING SYSTEM FOR QUALITY RISK ASSESSMENT:

On receipt of the quality risk assessment form, QA shall assign the number and the same shall be
entered in “Quality Risk Assessment Log” as per format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F03.

Numbering system for Quality Risk Assessment as follows; RA/KXX/Y Y/ZZZ-RBB

Where,

‘RA’ stands for Risk Assessment

‘XXX? stand for department code as per SOP No. SOP/DP/QA/001 (It shall be two digit or three
digit e.g. QA, QC, EHS etc.)

YY’ stands for last two digit of financial year (financial year 2022 shall be mentioned as 22)

‘ZZZ’ stands for department wise sequentially increasing number starts from “001”.

RBB R stand for revision and BB stand for sequential revision number start from 00, 01, 02 and so

on.
For example, the first risk assessment raised by QA department in Year 2022, then it shall be

numbered as ‘RA/QA/22/001-R00’.

On completion of risk assessment, pre-approval shall be taken from as reviewed by from risk
assessment team and approval from QA Head/Designee. Risk accept or reject and their
control/mitigation shall be reviewed during the pre-approval.

If accepted, the risk assessment form shall be sent to the user department to proceed the risk
control/ mitigation.

In case the risk assessment is rejected, the same shall be communicated to concern departments and
risk assessment considered as “Closed” and kept in risk assessment file for future reference.

Risk assessment shall be reviewed by risk assessment team for proposed risk control and forward
to QA Head/Designee for approval.

Risk reduction includes:
Use the appropriate quality tool for the mitigation of risk or completion of the action item.
Preference should be given to reduce the occurrence rather than increasing the levels of detection.

Process that improve the detectability of hazards and quality risks might also be used as part of a
risk control strategy.

Implementation of risk reduction measures should not introduce new risks into the system or

increase the significance of other existing risks, it is required to revisit the risk assessment to
identify and evaluate any possible change in the risk after implementing a risk reduction process
