74 Risk Control — Mitigation plan, Risk reduction or Acceptance

TAA] Risk control includes decision making to reduce and/or accept risks. The purpose of risk control is
to reduce the risk to an acceptable level.

7.4.2 Risk control might focus on the following questions:

7.4.2.1 Is the risk above an acceptable level?

7.4.2.2 | What can be done to reduce or eliminate risks?

7.4.2.3 What is the appropriate balance among benefits, risks and resources?

7.4.2.4 Are new risks introduced as a result of the identified risks being controlled?

74.3 The Mitigation plan elaborates the current control and the actions to reduce the overall risk to an
acceptable levels or elimination.

TAA For cases where overall risk is “Low”, mitigation plan is not required and the risk assessment shall

be closed.

7.4.5 The mitigation plan is mandatory where overall risk is “Medium” and "High", controls relating to
the risk should be implemented as soon as practically possible.

7.4.6 — Mitigation plan for medium category risk shall be done to reduce the risk to an acceptable level.

7.4.7 Mitigation plan for high category risk shall be Identify and implement first to reduce the risk to an
acceptable level, before proceeding to the activity.

74.8 The mitigation plan shall briefly explain the recommended action to be taken; responsible person
shall be implementing the controls mentioned in the risk mitigation plan and the expected target
date for completion.
