5.17

Risk Priority Number (RPN):
A quantitative method for determining the level of risk by multiplying the severity, probability of
occurrence and detectability rankings of the failure or event.

5.18 Risk Priority Ranking (RPR):
A qualitative method for determining the level of risk by combining severity, occurrence and
detectability rankings of the failure or event.

5.19 Residual Risk:
Risk remaining after risk control measures has been taken.

5.20 Validation:
Establishing documented evidence that provides a high degree of assurance that a specific process
will consistently produce a product meeting its predetermined specifications and quality attributes.

5.21 Direct Impact System:
Those Building /Facility / Utility / Equipment / Instrument / Process which controls and or sustain
a cGMP operation directly thereby impacting safety, efficacy and or purity of a product. (example :
Manufacturing block, Clean room, Facility to ensure flow of material and personnel to minimize
contamination, dedicated facilities / equipment, Purified water system, Filling machine, AHU
system for areas where product / materials are exposed during operation.)

5.22 Indirect Impact Systems:
Those Building / Facility / Utility / Equipment / Instrument / Process which do not control and or
sustain a cGMP operation directly, but still perceived to have an impact on safety, efficacy and or
purity of a product (e.g. : Boiler steam, Raw water system, Building Management System, access
control etc.).

5.23 Qualitative Risk Assessment (Informal):
Qualitative risk assessment shall be performed based on the level of risk ranking, as high, medium
and low. Risk priority ranking shall be evaluated in qualitative risk assessment.

5.24 Quantitative Risk Assessment (Formal):
Quantitative risk assessment shall be performed based on the level of risk rating. Risk priority
number shall be calculated in quantitative risk assessment.
