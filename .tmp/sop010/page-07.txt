Scope: State the applicability of risk assessment including facility and company name.

System/ Equipment/ Instrument/ Other (if any) Overview: Brief description of System/ Equipment/
Instrument/ Other including functions, intended use, component details, process flow etc.

Procedure: General process flow of quality risk management as follows,

Define the problem and/or risk question, including pertinent assumptions identifying the potential
for risk.

Assemble background information and/ or data on the potential hazard, harm or human health
impact relevant to the risk assessment.

Identify a leader and necessary resources.

Specify a timeline, deliverables and appropriate level of decision making for the risk management

process.
Flow chart for risk assessment shall be as per annexure no. SOP/DP/QA/010/A01.

Risk Assessment Team: User Head/Designee along with QA consultation shall form the team for
discussion on risk assessment and identify the potential failure, their control and mitigation plan (if
applicable).

During risk assessment team meeting, minutes of meeting as identified points of risk assessment
shall be mentioned in format for “Minutes of Meeting for Risk Assessment” SOP/DP/QA/010/F01
or if required minutes of meeting shall be prepared in word format as per format no.
SOP/DP/QA/010/FO1 and detail risk assessment shall be prepared by iniating department as per
format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F02 with reference of points identified during meeting. Additional
points (risk failure) may be added during actual preparation of risk.

Format for “Minutes of Meeting for Risk Assessment” SOP/DP/QA/010/F01 shall be requested to
QA as per SOP No. SOP/DP/QA/003 by user department.

Risk Identification: Mention the idenfied risk considering with process/activity and their potential
failure.

Risk assessment measure (Informal or formal risk assessment): During meeting, user department
along risk assessment team shall perform the evaluation of questions identified as per format no.
SOP/DP/QA/010/F01 and nature of risk assessment shall be decided as informal risk assessment
(Qualitative) or formal risk assessment (Quantitative).

Flow chart for decision (informal or formal risk assessment) shall be mentioned as per annexure
no. SOP/DP/QA/010/A02.
