Periodic review of Risk Assessment: -
As ongoing part of quality management process, applicable risk assessment and their outcome shall
be reviewed.

Review applicability of risk’s shall be considered/decided on nature of risk’s, potential impact and
effectiveness retention period of risk’s. Review applicability of risk’s shall be mentioned in risk
assessment format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F02, at the time of risk assessment closure.

Risk assessment shall be reviewed every two years. Review can be initiated one month before
completion of two years period.

Risk assessment review shall be done as per format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F06

Risk assessment revision shall be handled through change control procedure in case revision is
required as per review outcome of format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F06.

Risk assessment periodic review shall be done by respective user or
product/process/equipment/system owner.

7.6.3 Risk assessment review schedule shall be prepared yearly i.e. in the month of march of every
financial year or as and when required; schedule shall be prepared as per format no.
SOP/DP/QA/010/F05.

7.6.4 Risk assessment may be reviewed before periodic review in following circumstance (but not
limited to),

7.6.4.1 Impact of high level deviations.

7.6.4.2 Product performance (Product Recall, Field Alert).

7.6.4.3. Market Complaints.

7.6.4.4 Walk through/ internal audit/ External audit observations, Major changes impacting facility,
equipment or processes.

7.6.4.5 Annual Product Quality Review (APQR) recommendations.

77 Risk Closure:

TIA Once the risk review is completed by risk assessment team and risk is reduced to acceptable level
(if applicable), it shall be forwarded to QA Head/Designee for approval followed by closure in the
risk assessment log as per format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F03.
