5.0 DEFINITIONS:
5.1 Quality Risk Management:
A systematic process for the assessment, control, communication, implementation and review of
risks to the quality of the drug product across the product lifecycle.
5.2 Risk:
The combination of the probability of occurrence of harm and the severity of that harm.
5.3 Risk Assessment:
A systematic process of organizing information to support a risk decision. It consists of the
identification of hazards and the analysis and evaluation of risks associated with exposure to those
hazards.
