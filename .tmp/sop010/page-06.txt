7.0 PROCEDURE:
71 Risk Management Process involves the following steps :

7.1.1 Initiation of a quality risk management process
7.1.2 Team Selection
7.1.3 Risk Assessment — Identification, Analysis and Evaluation
7.1.4 Risk Control — Mitigation plan, Risk reduction or Acceptance
7.1.5 Risk Communication — QRM process shall be communicated between the team members
7.1.6 Risk Review
7.1.7. Risk Closure
7.2 Initiation of a quality risk management process:
7.2.1 Quality risk management should include systematic processes designed to coordinate, facilitate and
improve science-based decision making with respect to risk. Possible steps used to initiate and plan
a quality risk management process might include the following,
7.2.1.1 Risk assessment team shall be selected for quality risk management process. Risk assessment team
shall be made with subject matter experts to identify the risk and related controls by user
department Head/Designee in coordination with QA.
7.2.1.2 Meeting shall be arranged at initial to decide the strategy for risk assessment (Informal/Formal) and
to identify the probable failure. Outcome of meeting shall be recorded as per format for “Minutes
of Meeting for Risk Assessment” as per format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/FOL.
73 Risk Assessment — Identification, Analysis and Evaluation
7.3.1 Risk identification is a systematic use of information to identify hazards referring to the risk
question or problem description. Identified risk shall be mentioned in template for risk assessment
by FMEA method as per format no. SOP/DP/QA/010/F02.
7.3.2 Flow of Risk Assessment Template:
7.3.2.1 Pre-approval: Pre-approval of risk assessment shall be taken after identification of risk, their
control and before implementation of mitigation plan (if applicable).
7.3.2.2 Objective: State the objective of risk assessment including name of _ the
equipment/instrument/system, process, activity etc.
