Title: Quality Risk Assessment

Dept.: Quality Assurance | SOP No.: SOP/DP/QA/010 | Revision No: R04 Page 2 of 51

1.0 PURPOSE:

11 To provide a procedure to identify various potential risks to the design, facility, process, materials,
systems, equipment, utility etc. to assess / reduce quality risk and devise a systematic process for
the assessment, control, communication and review of risks to the quality of the medicinal product.

2.0 SCOPE:

2.1 This SOP is applicable to all the systems/equipment’s/instruments, process, procedure, facility and
utility, which have direct or indirect impact on the product quality at Drug Product Facility of M.J.
Biopharm Private Limited, Pune.

3.0 RESPONSIBILITY:

3.1 USER DEPARTMENT:

3.1.1 User department personnel shall identify, initiate, assess, control, implement and manage the risk.
3.1.2 Completion of the decided mitigation plan in their area (as applicable).

3.1.3. Review and evaluate the level of the risk.

3.1.4 Propose the appropriate control measure to mitigate the risks.

3.1.5 Implementation of the proposed control measures and closer of the risk identified.

3.2 CROSS FUNCTIONAL DEPARTMENT:

3.2.1. Evaluation of the risk identified with respect to their department functions, procedures and
documentation.
3.2.2 Completion of mitigation plan if evaluated as responsible department.
3.3 QUALITY ASSURANCE:
3.3.1 To prepare and review the SOP.
3.3.2 Site Quality Head/QA Head shall be responsible for approval of this SOP
3.3.3 Evaluation of the risk identified.
3.3.4 Assign the risk identification number.
3.3.5. Review the risk mitigation action plan for each identified risk.
